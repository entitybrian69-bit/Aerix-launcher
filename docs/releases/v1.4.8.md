# Aerix Launcher 1.4.8 — real footage, in the launcher

## Ten real Minecraft animations, installed

The Animations tab now opens with **ten real recorded clips** — not drawn, not procedural. A lakeside
Minecraft world, rendered at 720p, filmed through a whole day and its weather:

| | |
|---|---|
| Clear Day | sun on the lake, clouds drifting |
| Drifting Cloud | overcast, slow cloud movement |
| Rain on the Lake | falling rain and surface ripples |
| First Snow | heavy snowfall over the water |
| High Noon | bright midday light |
| Blue Hour | dusk, sky deepening |
| Lantern Glow | midnight, the lamp lit on the shore |
| Still Night | calm night clouds |
| Night Rain | rain in the dark |
| Silent Snowfall | snow at midnight |

Each is a **ten second seamless loop**, muted, around 0.1–2.4 MB, and the whole set adds about
**7 MB** to the APK. They are played by the launcher itself, so they need no download, no plugin and
no internet.

## They fill the screen

The player now uses a cropped video surface instead of a letterboxed one, so a 16:9 clip covers a
tall phone screen completely — no black bars down the sides. Tapping a clip applies its palette to
the interface as its accent colour, the same way a photographic wallpaper does.

## Where they came from

The footage is by **Blaž Jerman**, licensed **CC BY-SA 4.0**. The credit is shown on the wallpapers
page, as the licence requires. If you would rather ship without third-party footage, say so and I
will pull these ten and keep your own imports.

## Your own clips still work

**Add animation** still takes any video or animated GIF/WebP from your device, and those appear
alongside the ten bundled ones.

## Installing

Install over your existing Aerix app; **do not uninstall or clear data**.
