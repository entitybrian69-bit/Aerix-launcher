# Aerix Launcher 1.4.7 — your own real animations, and blocky scenes

## Import real animations — as many as you want

The **Add animation** button now takes anything Android can play: **video clips** (MP4, WebM, MKV,
M4V, MOV) and **animated GIF or WebP** files. Each one is copied into the app's own storage, so:

- you can keep **as many as you like** — they appear as cards in the Animations grid, showing a frame
  from the clip itself;
- they **loop forever**, so a four second clip looks continuous;
- they are **tiny** — a two second looping WebP or GIF is often a few hundred kilobytes, and a short
  MP4 a couple of megabytes;
- they cost the APK **nothing**, because they live in your storage, not in the download;
- tap a card to apply, tap × to remove.

This is the honest route to genuinely animated Minecraft footage: recorded clips are the one thing
that cannot be drawn in code, and no library of them can legally ship inside the app.

## The drawn scenes look like Minecraft now

The 25 built-in scenes no longer use smooth gradient hills:

- **blocky terrain** — every column is one block wide and its height snaps to whole blocks, so the
  ground reads as cubes;
- **trees** — two-block trunks under square canopies that lean with the wind;
- **clouds** — flat slabs of blocks sliding across the sky at their own speeds, as in game;
- **square sun and moon**, because Minecraft's own light is a square texture, not a disc.

## Also

- Motion off now freezes imported clips on their first frame, matching the rest of the launcher.

## Installing

Install over your existing Aerix app; **do not uninstall or clear data**.
