# Aerix Launcher 1.4.4 — the wallpaper moves, the chrome turns to water

## The picture itself moves

A still photo cannot have its trees sway, but it can stop behaving like a still photo. The wallpaper
is no longer drawn as one flat image:

- **Every strip of it moves.** The picture is drawn as narrow columns, each nudged on its own by a
  slow wave, so the whole frame shimmers — water, haze, moving air — instead of sliding around as
  one rigid rectangle. This works on every Android version, because it is plain drawing rather than
  a shader.
- **It breathes and pans.** The image slowly zooms and drifts, so the composition itself moves while
  its parts move against each other.
- **Light sweeps across it.** Two washes drift in opposite corners like a moving sun, a band of light
  crosses the frame and comes back, and the theme accent breathes underneath.
- **More of everything floating.** Petals, embers, snow, stars, fireflies, bubbles and motes now come
  in heavier, layered numbers, and every one of the 25 wallpapers still picks its own.

None of it was added to the APK: the effect is derived from the wallpaper you chose.

## The launcher you can see through

- Every panel, card and bar is **dramatically more transparent** — roughly half of what it was — so
  the moving wallpaper is visible behind the interface instead of a flat sheet of colour over it.
- Text and icons keep their weight and contrast, and the Dim control still lets you trade clarity
  for legibility in a bright room.

## A cleaner home page

- The big hero banner is **gone**. The active world now opens the page, with the task summary and
  quick tools beside it, quick actions under them, and recent worlds below — everything moved up
  into the space the banner was taking.
- **Play is still one tap away** on the active world panel.

## A cleaner wallpapers page

- The settings tab strip is **gone from this page** — it is a full page reached from its own rail
  entry, so it starts with its own controls. (The rail's Settings entry still returns you to the
  settings tabs.)
- **Motion and Blur now live in the top bar**, on the left, with the dim control and the Solid Dark
  and Custom buttons on the right. One bar instead of two, and the grid of wallpapers starts higher.
- The Motion button in that bar and the Animated theme switch in Settings are the same setting:
  turning either off stops the drift, the light, the particles and the accent glow at once, with no
  restart.

## Installing

Install over your existing Aerix app; **do not uninstall or clear data**. Signed with the same key as
1.4.3, so it updates in place.
