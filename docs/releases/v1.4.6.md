# Aerix Launcher 1.4.6 — no more strips, real scene previews, and video wallpapers

## The strips are gone

Moving the wallpaper in vertical columns left visible seams across the picture. That approach is
removed: the wallpaper now moves as **one whole frame** — a slow breathing zoom with a gentle pan —
and the motion on top of it comes from the light and particle layers instead of cutting the picture
up.

## The cards show the real scenes

The animation grid used to preview each scene as a couple of flat colour blocks. Each card now paints
**the actual scene** — its sky, its own light, its hills and its water — at a fixed moment, so what
you see in the grid is what you get behind the launcher.

## Video wallpapers: real footage

**Add video** on the Animations tab lets you pick any clip from your device (MP4, WebM — anything
Android can play). It plays full screen behind the interface, muted, looping seamlessly, and it stops
the moment the launcher leaves the screen.

- It costs the APK nothing: the clip is copied into the app's own storage, not into the download.
- The Motion switch still means something here: off holds the clip on its first frame.
- This is the only kind of wallpaper that is genuinely recorded footage rather than something drawn,
  which is why it is a separate option rather than a replacement for the 25 scenes.

## Installing

Install over your existing Aerix app; **do not uninstall or clear data**. Signed with the same key as
1.4.5, so it updates in place.
