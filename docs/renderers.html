Renderers

Picked per version, not per launcher

Minecraft on Android lives or dies by its renderer. Aerix chooses one for the version you are launching, and leaves every option one tap away.

What the screen gives you

  • Renderer per instance, with a recommended default.
  • Vulkan driver and graphics API chosen alongside it.
  • LTW for 1.17+ and LTW Legacy for 1.8–1.16.5, both built from source in this repository.
  • MobileGlues for 1.17+ and two VGPU options for 1.16.5 and older.

The lineup

Every backend, and where it fits

Ranges are the Minecraft versions each renderer is meant for. MobileGlues and the VGPU pair ship with the launcher; LTW and LTW Legacy are built from its source.
