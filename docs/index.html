What 1.5.6 includes

Every release writes its own log, and this is the current one — the change you can feel first, then the details that make it hold. The full history is in the release notes.

aerix@launcher:~$ release-notes --current v1.5.6
  1. 01 The first-run guide can no longer close the launcher The tour positioned its card from the component it was pointing at. When that component was off screen the offset went negative, and Compose rejects a negative padding — first launch died on AerixGuide.kt:235. The anchor is now validated and clipped before anything is drawn.
  2. 02 A step that points at nothing falls back to the centred card A rail item left of the overlay, a page still sliding in, a card already scrolled away — the tour keeps going instead of pointing at empty space.
  3. 03 Every padding is clamped Finite, never negative, never larger than the space it is applied to. Unbounded and zero-size measurements are handled instead of divided against, so no future component can take a launch down either.

The stand-out feature

Controls that look like something

Open the control editor and scroll to Control themes. The strip is the artwork itself: tap one and the control wears it, with its own press animation, haptic tick and key sound. Off puts the plain control back, and one button applies the theme you picked to every control in the layout.

Move it freezes the motion if you would rather have a still themed control; Feel it and Hear it switch the haptics and the click. Themes only ever change paint — a key press is still sent on the frame your finger lands, so nothing is delayed.

Free, and no account to make

Aerix has no sign-up of its own. Bring a Microsoft account, an Ely.by account, or play offline — nothing about you is sent anywhere but the login you chose.

Your data stays on your phone

Instances, worlds, accounts and settings all live in the launcher's own storage. No telemetry, no analytics, no ads.

Updates itself

The in-app updater reads the newest release, picks the build that matches your device, and installs over the old one without touching your data.
